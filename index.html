Inside the Pyramids

What about hieroglyphs?

The five 4th-Dynasty pyramids here have no carved hieroglyphic texts on their inside walls; what survives in them are builders’ marks and later inscriptions, shown as clay diamonds. Inscribed burial chambers first appear about 150 years after Menkaure, in the Pyramid of Unas at Saqqara (c. 2350 BC). Open it to see where its Pyramid Texts are, shown in blue.
